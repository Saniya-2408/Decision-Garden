🌱 How it started

Born from burnout, not hustle culture

In late 2022, our founder Arjun was staring at a 47-item to-do list at 9am on a Monday, feeling completely paralysed. Not because the tasks were hard — but because he had no idea which one to start with given how tired he felt that morning.

He tried every productivity app on the market. They all had the same flaw: they treated every task as equally urgent, every hour as equally productive. They ignored the most important variable — you.

Decision Planner was built on a simple idea: your tasks should adapt to your energy, not the other way around. And the process of planning should feel like tending a garden — slow, intentional, and deeply satisfying.

Today, over 12,000 people use Decision Planner to reclaim focus, reduce decision fatigue, and find joy in their daily work. We're just getting started.

🌻 What we believe

Our values

🌿
Energy is everything

Your capacity changes every day. The best task system respects that — and adapts intelligently instead of demanding constant peak performance.

🎨
Beauty matters

Tools you love to open get used. We invest deeply in making Decision Planner visually delightful, because aesthetics and function are not opposites.

🔒
Your data is yours

We're offline-first by design. Your tasks live on your device. No surveillance, no selling data, no cloud lock-in. Privacy is a feature, not a footnote.

🌱
Progress over perfection

A small step forward every day beats a perfect system you never use. Our garden metaphor celebrates consistency, not intensity.

💚
Sustainable productivity

We don't gamify burnout. Decision Planner gently nudges, never demands. The goal is a life well-lived, not a to-do list fully cleared.

🤝
Community-first

Our roadmap is shaped by real user feedback. Every major feature started as a conversation with a gardener who needed something better.
