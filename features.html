Feature 01

Garden Visualization

Every task is a plant in your personal garden. Blooming tasks glow with color and life. Neglected ones wilt gently. Completed tasks become flowers. Progress has never been so beautiful — or so emotionally resonant.

  • Tasks visually bloom as you make progress on them
  • Wilting animation for tasks sitting idle too long
  • Four growth stages: seed → growing → blooming → harvested
  • Color-coded by category (work, health, creative, personal)
  • Click any plant to view task details and update progress
  • 7-day streak tracking with visual milestone rewards

Feature 02

Smart Filtering Engine

Set your energy, mood, and available time — the engine does the rest. Real-time suggestions surface the tasks most aligned to your current capacity, so you always start with momentum.

  • Energy-level matching: High / Medium / Low
  • Mood filters: Focused, Creative, Routine, Social
  • Duration constraints so you don't overcommit
  • Category and priority layered filtering
  • Fit score (0–99) shows match quality instantly
  • One-click "Start now" or "Plant in garden"

Feature 03

Drag & Drop Planning Board

A Kanban board designed for your whole life — not just work. Move tasks between stages, prioritize your day, and see your projects come together. Intuitive, tactile, and deeply satisfying.

  • Backlog → To Do → In Progress → Review → Done
  • Drag between columns with smooth animations
  • Due dates and priority indicators on every card
  • Progress bars show completion percentage
  • Category color-coding for instant visual scanning
  • Create custom projects for different areas of life
